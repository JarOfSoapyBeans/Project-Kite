Cookies Kept on your Kite backend, never in this browser.

Sign in to a site in Kite by bringing its cookies from your normal browser. There, install a cookie export extension (for example Cookie-Editor), open the site while signed in (e.g. youtube.com), export its cookies as JSON (or a cookies.txt file), then paste or choose the file here. Treat an export like a password: anyone with it is signed in as you.
